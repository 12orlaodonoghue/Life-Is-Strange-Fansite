Welcome to Arcadia Bay

Life Is Strange is a 2015 episodic, story-based game by Dontnod Entertainment. In this game, you play as Max Caulfield, An 18-year old photography student who can rewind time!

Set in the fictional coastal town of Arcadia Bay, this story features themes of nostalgia, growing up, friendship and destiny.

With a cosy hand-painted artstyle, acoustic, indie soundtrack and warm golden lighting, playing this game feels like a warm hug.

The Story

Max Caulfield returns to her hometown after 5 years to study photography at Blackwell Academy. Her story changes forever when she discovers her ability to rewind time

Max has a vision in class of a tornado that will destroy her town in 5 days. Reunited with her childhood best friend Chloe Price, together they investigate Max's powers, the storm and search for missing girl Rachel Amber

The butterfly effect has a major impact on the game as your choices will influence conversations, relationships and scenarios.

Why this story is so special to me

  • The power to rewind time to see how different choices will play out
  • Playing detective to solve the many mysteries of Arcadia Bay
  • Getting to know complicated characters and navigating relationships

Warning! This game tackles heavy themes such as mental health, bullying and loss

The Characters

Max Caulfield

An introverted geek who loves photography and journalling. Shaped by trying to help everyone, she throughout the story she transforms into the "everyday hero" of Arcadia Bay.

Chloe Price

Chloe is Max's blue-haired, rebellious but fiercely loyal best friend. Despite being angry at the world since losing her dad at 14,due to her dynamic with Max punk-style and humour she is a fan-favourite character.

The Atmosphere

Slow-paced, quiet interactions within the world of the game like sitting on a bench or taking a scenic photo create a comforting, intimate atmosphere.

Environment

Life Is Strange is set in Oregon. The tired seaside town setting features rustic architecture, an old-fashioned American diner and foggy mornings.

Colour and Lighting

The events of the game take place in early October 2013. In the early chapters of the game, scenes are bathed in warm golden sunlight. This contrasts with the later chapters where darker colours represent danger and mystery.

Details

The characters' spaces teach us so much about them. Max's dorm room is filled with photos, string lights and analog technology while Chloe's bedroom is messy and filled with graffiti and posters.

Music

The Life Is Strange soundtrack is considered to be the backbone of the game's success. The original score was composed by Jonathan Morali of the French indie-folk band 'Syd Matters'. The gentle, minimalist tunes embody the mindful, introspective journey of the story.

What Makes Life Is Strange's Atmosphere One of a Kind

  1. Moments of Calm Chances for quiet reflection
  2. Soothing Songs Sets emotional tone and reflects the games themes
  3. Picturesque Environment Autumnal colours like orange leaves and coastal fog capture Max's nostalgia for the town
